How this works
  1. Open a stop and walk to the red mark on its map. The map is turned to the street grid: Riverside Drive runs up the right side and the river is to the left.
  2. Answer the questions by tapping. Where OpenStreetMap already has an answer it is shown under the question: check it and tap what you actually see. If something doesn't match the map, say so in the note.
  3. Photos: use your phone's camera app (one wide, one close-up) and put the stop number, e.g. S12, in the note. Then tap Save answer.

The numbered Entrance stops are places where a mapped path meets the street, a sidewalk or a crossing. They have not been checked: say whether you can actually get into the park there and what you pass through. They run along the park edge, so you can do them as a separate walk.

Stop numbers match the printed route sheet and map. Stops 1–5 are done on this page, 6–10 in MapComplete, 11–12 on whichever you prefer. Street names are the nearest cross street and are approximate north of 122nd. Nothing is changed in OpenStreetMap until an editor reviews your answers.

Answers are sent as you save them and also kept on this phone. If the end of the morning comes and anything shows as unsent, tap Send unsent, or Copy all answers and paste them into a message.
